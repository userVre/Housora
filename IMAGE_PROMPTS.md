# Housora image prompt pack

Use these prompts for clean, photorealistic architectural images. Unless a prompt says otherwise, use: no people, no text, no logos, no watermark, realistic materials, accurate perspective, natural lighting, editorial interior-design photography, high detail, 16:10 landscape crop for headers/cards, and keep the main subject centered enough for `object-fit: cover`.
